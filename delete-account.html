Pet Care World — Account deletion / Brisanje naloga

Request deletion without reinstalling the app

Email jalejasko011@gmail.com with the subject “Pet Care World — account deletion”. Include the email address of the account. Never send your password. Sending an email opens a support request; it is not an automatic deletion confirmation. We may verify ownership and will explain the processing.

In the app: Family overview → Family account → Internet account (optional) → Delete online account. Confirm with your password. The server removes sign-in, your family membership, invitations and reports associated with your account. A family with other members remains and ownership is transferred. An empty family is deleted. Minimal completed-request records are retained up to 30 days; unfinished requests remain queued until processed.

Local family profiles and shared pet records on a phone are separate from online accounts. Delete pets and reminders in the app. Clearing Android app storage removes all local profiles on that phone. For an anonymous AI report, include its receipt number in your deletion request. See the privacy policy for provider records and retention details.

Zahtev za brisanje bez ponovne instalacije

Pošaljite zahtev na jalejasko011@gmail.com, sa naslovom „Pet Care World — brisanje naloga“. Navedite e-mail naloga; nikada ne šaljite lozinku. E-mail pokreće zahtev podršci, a ne automatsku potvrdu brisanja. Možemo proveriti vlasništvo i objasniti tok obrade.

U aplikaciji: Porodični pregled → Porodični nalog → Internet nalog (opciono) → Obriši internet nalog. Potvrdite lozinkom. Brišu se prijava, vaše članstvo, pozivnice i prijave vezane za internet nalog. Ako postoje drugi članovi, porodica ostaje i vlasništvo se prenosi; prazna porodica se briše. Minimalna evidencija završenog zahteva čuva se do 30 dana. Nezavršeni zahtevi ostaju u redu do obrade.

Lokalni profili i zajednički podaci na telefonu su odvojeni. Ljubimce i podsetnike brišete u aplikaciji. Brisanje Android podataka aplikacije uklanja sve lokalne profile na tom telefonu. Za anonimnu AI prijavu navedite njen broj. Detalji su u politici privatnosti.
