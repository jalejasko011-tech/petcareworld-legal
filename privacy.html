PRIVACY POLICY — PET CARE WORLD
Date: 1 November 2026
Privacy, support and deletion contact: jalejasko011@gmail.com

This policy describes local pet care and optional online features in Pet Care World. Before public release, the app owner must confirm this text, the contact address, intended audience and retention configuration.

1. Information on your phone
Pet names, species, breeds, photos, pet health details, notes, feeding status and reminders are stored in the app’s private space on your phone. Local family profiles store a nickname and a salted password hash, not the password itself. Local profiles share the same pet data; they are not separate encrypted databases. This version does not automatically synchronize that data between phones. System backups depend on Android and device-account settings.

2. Online accounts and families
If you choose an online account, Google Firebase Authentication processes your email address, authentication information and account identifier. The app does not store a plaintext password. Firebase Cloud Firestore stores family membership, roles, joining times and invitations. These records support online family management; sharing local pets and feeding status between phones is not enabled in this version.

3. Location and nearby services
Approximate location is requested only when you choose that option. There is no background location tracking. You can enter a city instead. Geocoding, nearby searches and map providers process the selected area. OpenStreetMap/Overpass and map servers receive area or map-tile requests and network information such as an IP address. If you choose directions, the destination is passed to your selected navigation service, including Google Maps. Results may be incomplete. Check opening hours and availability directly with the provider.

4. AI assistant
With your consent, your questions and limited recent context from the selected conversation are sent to Google Firebase AI Logic/Gemini. Local profiles and pet health records are not automatically added to AI messages. Conversations and unfinished messages are saved in the private app storage on this phone until you delete them from the Conversations list. Starting a new conversation keeps the previous topic. Conversations are shared across local family profiles and online accounts on this phone, so anyone using the app on this phone can read them. They are not automatically synced between phones. Google processes requests under its terms and policies; clearing the local conversation does not automatically erase provider-side records. Do not submit human health, financial or other sensitive personal information. AI can be wrong and does not replace a veterinarian. In an emergency, contact a veterinarian immediately.

5. Reporting AI replies
Reports are voluntary. After confirmation, only the selected AI reply, reason, optional comment and language are sent, not the entire conversation. To limit excessive submissions, the app creates a random reporting identifier on that phone; the server uses its hash. If you are signed into an online account, the report is linked to that account identifier. Reports are reviewed for quality and safety, not marketing. Reports are retained for up to 30 days and technical rate-limit counters for up to 2 days, with a short automatic-deletion delay. Keep the report receipt if you want to request its deletion. Do not include personal information in your comment.

6. Providers and security
Optional online features use Google Firebase Authentication, Firestore, AI Logic, App Check/Play Integrity and Cloud Functions. These providers may process technical identifiers, IP addresses, app integrity signals and operational records to deliver services and prevent abuse. This version does not add an advertising SDK, usage analytics or Crashlytics. Global error handling shows a recovery screen without sending a crash report. Connections to our online services use HTTPS. We do not sell your data.

7. Deletion and retention
You can delete pets and reminders in the app. Delete an online account using “Delete online account”, or email the contact above with the subject “Pet Care World — account deletion”. You do not need to reinstall the app to make an external request. We may verify account ownership to protect against unauthorized deletion; never send your password by email.
Online deletion removes Firebase sign-in, your personal family link, your invitations and reports associated with that account. A shared family with remaining members is preserved and ownership is transferred to another member. If there are no remaining members, the family and its data are deleted. A minimal completed-request record is retained for up to 30 days for reliability and security. Unfinished requests remain queued until processed. Online deletion does not erase unrelated shared local records belonging to household members on a phone. Delete local records separately in the app; clearing Android app storage removes every local profile’s data on that phone.
We respond within a reasonable period and explain any ownership verification and expected processing. Provider-side operational record retention also depends on Google’s terms, configuration and applicable obligations.

8. Children and family use
The intended audience and access to online features require careful review. Parents should supervise use and prevent a child’s personal information from being entered into online accounts, location searches, comments or AI messages. This policy is not a statement that every online provider is approved for children’s independent use. Compliance with Google Play Families policies and provider age requirements must be confirmed before a child-directed public release. Contact us if you believe a child’s information was submitted without appropriate consent so we can investigate and delete it.

9. Choices and changes
An online account, approximate location, AI chat and AI reporting are not required for basic local pet care. Revoke permissions in Android settings. Contact us to ask questions or request correction or deletion. Material policy changes will be published with an updated date.
